# Steady state: after the app's startup `PRAGMA optimize`

Both runs seed 5 AI feeds with about 300 sparse matches each (one article in 667). Before the fixes recorded in PLAN D62 the AI feed page was a per-row `EXISTS` (951 ms with no statistics) and the nav counted matches the same way (about 30 ms extra). It now resolves match ids first and checks them against `UserArticles` with an inline id list.
